How many cycles does this engine have left?

Pick one of the 100 NASA test engines. Four regression models read its sensors and estimate the operating cycles remaining before failure. NASA recorded the true value, so you can see how close each model gets.

Its last observed cycle is loaded with the true remaining life NASA recorded.

Where the sensors sit on the engine

A simplified section of a two-spool turbofan, drawn to show the engine parts the data comes from. The orange numbers are NASA sensor numbers.

  • 2 LPC outlet temperature
  • 3 HPC outlet temperature
  • 4 LPT outlet temperature
  • 7 HPC outlet pressure

Fan, LPC and HPC are the fan and the low- and high-pressure compressors. HPT and LPT are the high- and low-pressure turbines.

Where engine health matters in flight

The NASA data simulates large turbofans, the engines of the first two aircraft below. Estimating remaining life applies to every engine in a fleet, but helicopters and drones would need their own data and models.
